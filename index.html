About this data Winners come from the Texas Secretary of State's returns for the November 3, 2026 general election, retrieved November 12, 2026. Results are unofficial until canvassed. Members elected in 2026 take office when the Legislature convenes on January 12, 2027.

Districts are found by matching your address with the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder. If it cannot place your address, the tool falls back to your ZIP code, using the Texas Legislative Council's "ZIP Codes by District" reports, and shows every district that ZIP touches. You can double-check at Who Represents Me.

Email addresses for sitting members come from Open States. This site is not affiliated with the State of Texas. Messages are written and sent from your own email account. This site never sees them and stores nothing. Your address is sent only to the Census Bureau, to find your districts.
