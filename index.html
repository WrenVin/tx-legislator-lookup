Who represents me in the Texas Legislature?

Enter your ZIP code to find your Texas state representative and state senator, with their email addresses. Updated with the November 3, 2026 election results.

About this data. Winners come from the Texas Secretary of State's returns for the November 3, 2026 general election, retrieved November 12, 2026. Results are unofficial until canvassed. Members elected in 2026 take office when the Legislature convenes on January 12, 2027.

ZIP-to-district matches come from the Texas Legislative Council's "ZIP Codes by District" reports for the current House (PLANH2316) and Senate (PLANS2168) maps. Many ZIP codes span more than one district, so this tool shows every district a ZIP touches. To be certain, look up your street address at Who Represents Me.

Email addresses for sitting members come from Open States. This site is not affiliated with the State of Texas. It stores nothing and sends your ZIP code nowhere.
